# أقلامنا / Aqlamna — Launch & Growth Plan

*Drafted 2 Aug 2026. A working plan to go through when you're ready — not a to-do list to finish in one sitting.*

---

## The one-line pitch

**"Twine, but Arabic-first."**

Everyone in interactive fiction knows Twine and Ink; none of them are RTL-native or Arabic-first. That single line opens every door below. Aqlamna is the first free, open-source, offline, Arabic-first tool for writing and playing interactive fiction — with a quality engine built from a real Arabic teacher's corrections.

---

## Where things stand (already done)

- ✅ Editor live at **aqlamna.org** (write → play → export standalone HTML, offline).
- ✅ Three finished stories: **حرّاس الخزنة** (flagship, Petra adventure, 4 endings), **العطر المفقود**, **طائرة الورق**.
- ✅ **"قصص جاهزة" showcase** on the landing page — visitors can play a full story in one click.
- ✅ Character-continuity, after-choice rendering, and AI-provider bugs fixed and deployed.
- ✅ English edition of حرّاس الخزنة ("Guardians of the Treasury") for non-Arabic readers.

**The gap:** nothing yet that makes a stranger *stop and try it*. That's what the plan below is for.

---

## The strategy in one breath

Don't try to be famous everywhere at once. **Pick one beachhead — Arabic educators & kids' content — and go deep.** That's where Arabic-first is a *must-have*, not a nice-to-have, and teachers spread tools by word of mouth. You already have patient zero: **Elham**, the teacher who built the quality corpus. That is the story.

Then run two fronts in sequence: first the **maker / IF world** (for credibility and early buzz), then the **education wedge** (for real adoption and partnerships).

---

## Step 1 — The 30-second demo video (the anchor asset)

The single most shareable thing you can make. Screen recording, no talking needed. One take, ~2 minutes to record.

**Shot list:**
- **0–8s:** In the قصتك tab, type a scene title + two sentences into a fresh scene. (Show the form — no code.)
- **8–16s:** Add two choices, pick their destinations from the dropdown. Hit ✨ and draw one image so a picture pops in.
- **16–24s:** Click **شغّل** and tap through the choices — show it *playing* like a real story.
- **24–30s:** Click **تصدير**, then flash the showcase page with the three story covers. End on the aqlamna.org logo.

Add 3–4 on-screen captions (Claude can write these). This video is the hook for every post below.

## Step 2 — The launch (a week of small, well-placed moves)

Do these *after* the video and showcase are ready.

**Front A — the maker / interactive-fiction world (credibility + buzz):**
- **itch.io** — upload the three stories. It's the home of Twine/Ink games; free, instant audience that gets it. Set project kind to HTML.
- **The Interactive Fiction Community Forum (intfiction.org)** and **r/twinegames / r/interactivefiction** — post as "first Arabic-first, RTL-native IF tool, free & open-source." This crowd loves filling a gap they can't.
- **Show HN (Hacker News)** + **Product Hunt** — lead with the *engineering* angle: "I built an interactive-fiction engine that handles Arabic RTL/bidi correctly." The bidi bugs you fought (`>=` rendering as `=<`, welded paragraphs, the blank-editor cache traps) are catnip for that audience.

**Front B — the Arabic education wedge (adoption + partnerships):**
- **Little Thinking Minds** (Amman — same city as you, Arabic literacy for K–12). Get a coffee meeting. Most natural partner/pilot.
- **Lamsa** (Riyadh, biggest Arabic kids' learning platform) — content-partnership target.
- **KamKalima** (Lebanon, Arabic teacher tools for schools) — classroom fit.
- **Arabic teacher Facebook / Telegram groups** and homeschool communities — where Elham's peers are.
- **Teacher pilot:** Elham + 3–4 teachers each make one short story → testimonials + a "made with Aqlamna" gallery.

**Press (once the video + showcase are live):** Wamda, MENAbytes, StartupMGZN. A free open-source Arabic tool with a teacher-built quality engine is a real story.

**Heritage angle (bonus, thanks to the Petra story):** Jordan Tourism Board, the Petra museum / development authority, cultural NGOs — interactive Arabic heritage storytelling is a pitch they rarely hear.

**GitHub:** polish the README with the demo GIF, add topics (interactive-fiction, twine, arabic, rtl), ask early fans for stars.

## The Elham demo (run-of-show)

Goal: show her how the tool works, live, while screen-sharing — and that it produces *good Arabic*.

1. Build **اختيار سامي** live (the simple supermarket/discipline story — one setup, three choices, three endings). ~2 minutes.
2. Draw **one image** on the opening scene with ✨/ارسم — the "wow" moment.
3. Play it through a "good" ending and a "wrong" ending.
4. Flip to **حرّاس الخزنة** in another tab: "and here's what it grows into." The contrast (2-minute story → full illustrated adventure) is the whole pitch.

The اختيار سامي script is corpus-clean on purpose — she'll recognize her own rules in it. Pitch line for her: *"this lets you build interactive lessons and value-stories for kids, in proper Arabic."*

## The honest truth about "famous"

It comes from a **beachhead + word of mouth + a couple of well-placed launches** — not one viral moment. Sequence it: great playable stories on the homepage (done) → a maker-community launch for credibility → the education wedge for real, sticky adoption. You've built something no one else has. Now it's about putting it in front of the few hundred people who will *get it* first, and letting them carry it.
